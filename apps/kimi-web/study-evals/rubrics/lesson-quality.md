# Chinese lesson quality rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A severe fabrication fails the fixture even
when the numeric total would otherwise pass.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | Deterministic proxy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outline completeness | Meaningful source units disappear | Coverage exists but disposition is vague | Every declared anchor is taught or visibly deferred | `plan.json` anchor coverage |
| Logical progression | Disconnected or inverted moves | Mostly ordered with weak prerequisite handling | Objective, explanation, example, boundary, practice form a teachable sequence | Required section order and progressive plan |
| Source coverage | Important declared facts absent | Partial coverage | Required facts appear inside source-labelled evidence | Required source phrases |
| Factual fidelity | Unsupported or contradictory claims | Ambiguous attribution | Source facts match declared anchors; teaching examples are separate | Anchor allow-list and forbidden claims |
| Chinese readability | Translationese, filler, or mostly non-Chinese | Understandable but stiff | Natural, compact Chinese that explains difficult steps progressively | CJK ratio, section substance, filler patterns |
| Explanation quality | Slogan or unexplained definition | One useful explanation | Conditions, reasoning, and boundary are made explicit | Core/plain/boundary sections |
| Example quality | Invented example presented as source | Example labelled but weakly connected | Source example is traceable and transfer example is explicitly pedagogical | `data-evidence` markers |
| Cross-lesson repetition | Repeated long paragraphs | Some avoidable restatement | No copied introduction, definition, or recap | Repeated-block detection |
| Self-check alignment | Activity unrelated to objective | Topic matches but criterion is vague | Objective keyword and observable decision are tested with feedback | Objective keywords in objective and self-check |
| Unsupported-fact ratio | One or more severe unsupported facts | Attribution gaps remain | No forbidden claim and no invalid source anchor | Forbidden-claim/invalid-anchor counts |

## Severe failures

Any of these makes a fixture fail:

- an anchor not present in the fixture source;
- a fabricated number, threshold, page, quotation, actor, or outcome;
- a teaching example labelled as material evidence;
- omission of a declared high-value source anchor without visible deferral;
- a self-check whose expected answer contradicts the source;
- copied learner-facing paragraphs across lessons.

## Comparison rule

Record the exact fixtures, contract revision, command, and scoring method for
every result. Do not compare against another product without running the same
sources, tasks, rubric, and review procedure on both systems.
